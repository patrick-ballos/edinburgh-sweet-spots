Edinburgh

Overview & History

Edinburgh, the capital city of Scotland, is famous for its cobblestone streets, medieval Old Town, and majestic Edinburgh Castle perched atop an extinct volcano.

It is a vibrant cultural hub known for hosting world-famous festivals, cozy teahouses, artisanal bakeries, and stunning scenic views from Arthur's Seat.
