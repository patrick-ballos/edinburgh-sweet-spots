Pinto Art Museum

Overview & Vibe

Situated in Antipolo, Rizal, Pinto Art Museum is an open-air art museum complex featuring Greek Mediterranean-inspired white buildings surrounded by lush botanical gardens.

It houses an extensive collection of modern and contemporary Filipino art, sculpture galleries, peaceful courtyards, and cozy garden cafés.
