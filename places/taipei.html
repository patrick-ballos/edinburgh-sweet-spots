Taipei

Overview & Culture

Taipei is the energetic capital of Taiwan, seamlessly blending modern urban marvels like Taipei 101 with historic night markets and serene mountain trails.

It is renowned worldwide for its food culture—offering incredible boba milk tea, traditional pastries, night market snacks, and rich cultural temples.
